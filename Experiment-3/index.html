About the University

Building Engineers. Creating Innovators.

Delhi Technological University has a long history of providing high-quality technical education. The university promotes academic excellence, practical learning, research, entrepreneurship and technological innovation.

Our Vision

To become a world-class university through education, innovation, research and development in engineering and technology.

Research & Innovation

DTU encourages students and researchers to work on innovative technologies and interdisciplinary projects aimed at solving real-world problems.

Student Development

Academic Departments

Explore Our Departments

DTU consists of multiple departments offering specialised education and research opportunities across engineering, sciences and technology.

DEPARTMENT 01

Computer Science & Engineering

Focuses on computer programming, algorithms, artificial intelligence, machine learning, databases, computer networks, cybersecurity and software engineering.

DEPARTMENT 02

Information Technology

Covers modern computing technologies including information systems, networking, cloud computing, web technologies and software development.

DEPARTMENT 03

Software Engineering

Deals with systematic software development, testing, software architecture and design of reliable software systems.

DEPARTMENT 04

Electronics & Communication Engineering

Provides education in electronic circuits, communication systems, signal processing, embedded systems and VLSI.

DEPARTMENT 05

Electrical Engineering

Focuses on electrical machines, power systems, control engineering, power electronics and renewable energy.

DEPARTMENT 06

Mechanical Engineering

Covers manufacturing, machine design, thermodynamics, robotics, fluid mechanics and automobile technologies.

DEPARTMENT 07

Civil Engineering

Provides knowledge in structural engineering, transportation, environmental engineering and sustainable infrastructure.

DEPARTMENT 08

Chemical Engineering

Combines chemistry, mathematics and engineering principles for industrial processes, energy and material development.

DEPARTMENT 09

Applied Sciences

Supports engineering education through physics, chemistry, mathematics and other scientific disciplines.
