About the University

Building Engineers. Creating Innovators.

Delhi Technological University has a long history of providing high-quality technical education. The university promotes academic excellence, practical learning, research, entrepreneurship and technological innovation.

Our Vision

To become a world-class university through education, innovation, research and development in engineering and technology.

Research & Innovation

DTU encourages students and researchers to work on innovative technologies and interdisciplinary projects aimed at solving real-world problems.

Student Development

Technical societies, cultural clubs, sports, competitions and entrepreneurship initiatives provide students opportunities beyond academics.

Academic Departments

Explore Our Departments

DTU consists of multiple departments offering specialised education and research opportunities across engineering, sciences, technology and management.

DEPARTMENT 01

Computer Science & Engineering

Focuses on computer programming, algorithms, artificial intelligence, machine learning, databases, computer networks, cybersecurity and software engineering.

DEPARTMENT 02

Information Technology

Covers modern computing technologies including information systems, networking, cloud computing, web technologies, software development and data-driven applications.

DEPARTMENT 03

Software Engineering

Deals with systematic software development, software architecture, testing, project management and design of reliable, large-scale software systems.

DEPARTMENT 04

Electronics & Communication Engineering

Provides education in electronic circuits, communication systems, signal processing, embedded systems, VLSI and modern communication technologies.

DEPARTMENT 05

Electrical Engineering

Focuses on electrical machines, power systems, control engineering, power electronics, renewable energy and electrical technologies.

DEPARTMENT 06

Mechanical Engineering

Covers manufacturing, machine design, thermodynamics, robotics, fluid mechanics, automobile technologies and industrial systems.

DEPARTMENT 07

Civil Engineering

Provides knowledge in structural engineering, transportation, environmental engineering, construction technology and sustainable infrastructure.

DEPARTMENT 08

Chemical Engineering

Combines chemistry, mathematics and engineering principles for chemical processes, industrial systems, energy and material development.

DEPARTMENT 09

Applied Sciences

Supports engineering education through fundamental learning and research in physics, chemistry, mathematics and other scientific disciplines.
